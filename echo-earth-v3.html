How it works

  1. 1Set a baseline: today's traffic, trees, paving, rain and drainage.
  2. 2Change one or more inputs or add interventions, then run the simulation.
  3. 3Read the indicators, uncertainty ranges and plain-language explanation.
  4. 4Save scenarios, compare them, and export a report with its assumptions.

Environmental responsibility

ECHO EARTH supports early thinking, not engineering sign-off. Indicators are relative and illustrative until calibrated with local data and reviewed by qualified people.

Air quality

Traffic, street layout and weather drive local pollution. Trees can trap some particles but also block airflow in narrow streets.

Urban heat

Dark paving stores heat; shade and evaporation cool. Real cooling depends on species, irrigation and local climate.

Rainfall and flooding

Impervious ground sends more rain to drains at once. Terrain and drain condition matter and aren't modelled here.

Vegetation

Canopy takes years to mature, so the model scales tree benefit with the time horizon.

About & methodology

Model

  • Effective tree cover = tree % × (0.6 + 0.4 × min(years/10, 1)), capped at 100 − impervious %.
  • Air indicator = 100 × clamp(0.85 × traffic × (1 − 0.3 × trees) + 0.15 × impervious), where traffic = vehicles ÷ 60,000.
  • Heat indicator = 100 × (0.5 × impervious + 0.25 × traffic + 0.25 × (1 − trees)).
  • Runoff (mm/h) = rain × (0.1 + 0.8 × impervious) × (1 − 0.3 × trees). Flood susceptibility = 100 × r/(1+r), r = runoff ÷ drainage.
  • Interventions: green roofs −6% heat and +5 pts green; permeable paving −20% runoff; electric shift −30% traffic. These factors are illustrative, not sourced.
  • Uncertainty bands (air ±30%, heat ±20%, flood ±35%) are fixed illustrative ranges, not statistical intervals.

Estimates vs observations

Scenario results are estimates from your inputs. Live conditions come from external models. Neither is a field measurement.
